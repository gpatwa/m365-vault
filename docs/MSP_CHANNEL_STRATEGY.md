## 2. Current MSP Backup Landscape

### What MSPs Use Today

| Platform | Wholesale Cost | Strengths | Weaknesses |
|----------|---------------|-----------|------------|
| **Veeam** | $3-5/user | 25 restore methods, VM expertise | Complex setup, weak M365/Teams/Entra, unpredictable pricing at scale |
| **Datto (Backupify)** | $3-4/user | MSP-first, 3× daily snapshots | Acquisition uncertainty, pricing increasing, limited intelligence |
| **AvePoint** | $3-5/user | Deep M365 coverage, compliance | Expensive, inflexible pricing, slow initial seeding |
| **N-able Cove** | $2-4/user | Fast transfers, good monitoring | Limited workload coverage |
| **MSP360** | $2-3/user | Cost-effective, no vendor lock-in | Basic features, limited intelligence |
| **Acronis** | $3-5/user | Security bundle, ransomware protection | Complex licensing, heavy platform |

### What They All Lack

| Missing Capability | Impact |
|-------------------|--------|
| Context-aware recovery plans | MSPs can't show clients HOW recovery happens |
| Criticality scoring | All users treated equally — CEO and intern same priority |
| Entra ID config backup | Attackers disable MFA; no undo available |
| Recovery confidence score | Can't prove recoverability to client's auditors |
| Anomaly detection (built-in) | Ransomware detected after the fact, not during backup |
| Intelligence at $0 surcharge | Premium features locked behind enterprise tiers |

## 11. Risk Factors & Mitigation

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|--------|------------|
| MSPs slow to switch from Veeam/Datto | Low adoption | Free 3-month pilot, run alongside existing tool |
| White-label delay | MSPs need their brand | Prioritize in Q3 build, interim: minimal Shieldio branding |
| Support burden from MSP clients | Scale issue | L1/L2 handled by MSP, Shieldio handles L3 only. Error codes self-resolve most issues. |
| Microsoft Backup Storage API disruption | Commoditizes basic backup | Our intelligence layer is the moat — pivot to "intelligence on top of MSFT backup" |
| Competitor price match | Margin pressure | Volume discounts + intelligence differentiation. Hard to match $1.50 + free intelligence. |
| MSP consolidation (M&A) | Partner loss | Diversify across 25+ MSPs, no single MSP >15% of revenue |
