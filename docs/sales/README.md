### 1. Cold outreach
- **`outreach-templates.md`** — LinkedIn connection requests, vertical-specific intro messages, single follow-up. *Internal-only as a template; the personalized output is sent to a prospect.*

### 2. Pre-call prep
- **`founder-demo-script.md`** — Default 30-minute call structure with a tight discovery section. Open this 5 minutes before the call. *Internal only.*
- **`discovery-questions.md`** — Pick-list of ~50 questions across 9 categories. Use when you have time to tailor questions to the buyer. *Internal only.*

### 3. Live, during the call
- **`founder-demo-script.md`** — The 6-section flow with timed transitions and the URLs to open in tabs.
- **`objection-handling.md`** — Quick-reference patterns for the 8 most common objections, with proof URLs to send next. Glance at this during the call. *Internal only.*

### 4. Post-call follow-up (within 24 hours)

## Default founder-led sales flow

For a typical founder-led conversation from cold contact through second meeting:

1. **Cold contact**: pick the right `outreach-templates.md` template by vertical → personalize in 2 minutes → send.
2. **Reply received**: skim `discovery-questions.md`, mark 3–5 questions tailored to the buyer's vertical and likely role.
3. **First call (30 min)**: run `founder-demo-script.md` with the picked discovery questions. Keep `objection-handling.md` open in another tab for live reference.
4. **Within 2 hours after the call**: send `follow-up-email-templates.md` Template 1 (discovery follow-up). Link the most relevant scenario URL and `/overview`.
5. **Within 24 hours**: fill `recovery-brief-template.md` from your call notes. Send the filled brief to the prospect, or paste its sections into a richer follow-up email if a separate document feels heavy.
6. **If the buyer is in a regulated vertical**: attach the relevant vertical one-pager (`healthcare-`, `finance-`, or `legal-one-pager.md`).
7. **Second call — technical walkthrough (60 min)**: run the 60-minute extension in `founder-demo-script.md`, walking the scenario most relevant to their environment.
8. **Within 2 hours after the second call**: send `follow-up-email-templates.md` Template 2 (technical walkthrough follow-up). Update the recovery brief if anything material changed.
9. **Security or procurement review**: route artifact requests through `security@kavachiq.com`. After the review session, send `follow-up-email-templates.md` Template 3.
10. **Healthcare buyer with a compliance reviewer in scope**: forward `hipaa-aware-m365-recovery.md` to the named reviewer.

The whole loop above stays inside this folder plus the public site URLs. There is no other internal asset a founder needs to run an early sale.

## Maintenance

- **Keep all assets aligned with the live site.** If a section, scenario URL, capability name, or contact path shifts on `/welcome`, `/scenarios/*`, `/security`, `/overview`, or `/contact`, update the references across this folder so live conversations and the docs do not drift apart.
- **Stay on locked strategy.** Identity-first cyber recovery for Microsoft Entra and Microsoft 365. Do not reintroduce pricing-led positioning, free-tier hooks, anti-Microsoft hero framing, AI-feature branding, open-source/self-hosted positioning, or "certified" over-claims (we have mapped controls; formal artifacts route through `security@kavachiq.com`).
- **Review the folder once a quarter.** Drift creeps in through small additions. The `objection-handling.md` "What not to say" section, the `recovery-brief-template.md` "Things to never include" section, and the `follow-up-email-templates.md` "Things to avoid" section are the canonical guardrails — if any new asset contradicts them, the asset is wrong, not the guardrails.
